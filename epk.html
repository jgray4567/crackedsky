About the Band

Cracked Sky

A passionate and energetic 4-piece band from Manassas covering hard rock, grunge, and post-grunge.

Cracked Sky is a hard rock and grunge cover band built for audiences that crave high-energy 90s and 2000s alternative rock. With driving guitars, heavy bass, tight drumming, and powerful vocals, the band delivers a visceral live show that brings the era of grunge and post-grunge back to the stage.

Cracked Sky gives venues and event planners a heavy-hitting, professional live music option for rock clubs, outdoor festivals, and private events across the DMV. They deliver a passionate performance that gets crowds moving.

Whether it's a packed rock club or a festival main stage, Cracked Sky brings an unwavering commitment to the music, delivering the iconic sound of 90s alternative rock with authenticity and raw power.
